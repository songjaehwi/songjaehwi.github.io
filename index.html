About

I am a robotics researcher interested in embodied AI and robot learning. Currently, I work as a Robotics Intern at Config Intelligence and as a Research Intern at KAIST RISE Lab, advised by Prof. Kimin Lee.

I am pursuing a double major in Information Systems and Computer Science at Hanyang University, expecting to graduate in February 2026. My recent research experience includes working on Sim2Real deployment of quadruped robots at Korea Institute of Science and Technology (KIST) and semi-supervised learning at Hanyang University LRNING Lab.
