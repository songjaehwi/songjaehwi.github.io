About

I am an undergraduate student at Hanyang University. My current research interests focus on robot intelligence, with a particular emphasis on multi-view spatio-temporal reasoning and learning from human demonstrations. My long-term vision is to build robot agents that can autonomously reason, make decisions, and self-correct their behaviors. Currently, I work as a Research Intern at KAIST RISE Lab, advised by Prof. Kimin Lee. I also work as a Robotics Intern at Config Intelligence, supervised by Ph.D. Hyungmok Son and co-advised by Prof. Kimin Lee, Prof. Minjoon Seo, and Sungdong Kim.
