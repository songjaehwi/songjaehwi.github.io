Jaehwi Song

I am a Ph.D. Student at KAIST, advised by Prof. Kimin Lee. My long-term vision is to build robot agents that can naturally coexist and interact with humans while autonomously reasoning, making decisions, and self-correcting their behaviors. To this end, I am focusing on Human-Robot Interaction and learning from human demonstrations to address the robot data bottleneck for training Robot Foundation Models.

I also work as a Member of Technical Staff at Config, supervised by Ph.D. Hyungmok Son and co-advised by Prof. Kimin Lee, Prof. Minjoon Seo, and Sungdong Kim.
