# Institution logos

Local assets identify the institutions in Education and Experience. Wordmark PNGs retain their supplied artwork and are displayed proportionally with CSS; other PNGs are resized to at most 180px. SVGs retain their original artwork.

- `kaist-wordmark.png`: KAIST wordmark supplied by the site owner, used without image modifications.
- `kist-wordmark.png`: KIST signature requested by the site owner, from https://hdeori.github.io/assets/images/logos/logo-kist.png. A CSS viewport displays only the KIST mark, preserving the source artwork.

- `kaist.png`: https://www.kaist.ac.kr/common/img/apple-touch-icon.png
- `config.svg`: https://config.inc/brand/config-mark-black.svg (brand assets: https://config.inc/brand)
- `kist.png`: https://www.kist.re.kr/_res/kist/ko/img/common/logo_on.png
- `hanyang.png`: Hanyang University symbol from https://www.hanyang.ac.kr/en/web/eng/identity
- `rokaf.svg`: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Emblem_of_the_Republic_of_Korea_Air_Force.svg (Republic of Korea Air Force emblem; SVG by Lee6597; see source for attribution and reuse terms).

Names and marks belong to their respective institutions.
